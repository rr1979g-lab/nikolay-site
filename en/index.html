My name is Nikolay Shcherbakov. I write books about thinking, intuition, mindfulness, habits and personal change, and I create practical mobile apps.

My journey didn't begin with a wish to write books. It began with a wish to understand myself better — my thoughts, and what a person is truly capable of.

For more than 20 years I have been studying and practicing meditation, breathwork, affirmations, attention techniques and self-observation.

I have always been drawn to questions like these:

  • Why do some thoughts help us move forward, while others keep us trapped in endless doubt?
  • Why do we so often worry about a future that hasn't even arrived yet?
  • Can we learn to hear our intuition more clearly?
  • How do small daily actions gradually change our lives?

I didn't want to stop at beautiful theories. It mattered to me to test ideas against my own experience, watch the results, notice my mistakes and find what truly works in everyday life.

Why I started writing books

Over time I gathered observations, exercises and practical methods that I wanted to share.

That is how my books came about — on intuition, decision-making, affirmations, intrusive thoughts and fear of the future. Each book is devoted to one specific problem.

  • The Intuition Code helps you learn to notice the first inner signal.

  • The Right Choice — about the moments when fear and doubt get in the way of making a decision.

  • Destiny to Order shares my many years of experience working with affirmations.

  • Thoughts in Circles helps you relate differently to recurring thoughts, without getting pulled into an endless argument with them.

  • What If Everything Turns Out Well? — about anxiety over the future and the skill of bringing your attention back to the present.

My experience of changing how I eat holds a special place. For more than 15 years I have lived without many everyday foods. This path has given me a chance to observe how habits, well-being and my relationship with my own body change.

I don't write to promise instant transformation. I write to give people clear tools they can practice on their own.

Why I started creating apps

At some point I realized that reading a book isn't enough. We can learn a useful technique or feel inspired by a new idea, and a few days later slip back into old habits.

That's where the idea came from: mobile apps that help carry knowledge into everyday life. You don't need to reread a whole chapter every time to remember your breathing, your attention or how to make a decision. You can open the app, do a short practice and get on with your day.

  • MOMENT NOW — to bring your attention back to the present moment.

  • BREATH PRANA MOMENT — to use breathing practices to relax and restore a sense of calm.

  • INTUITION TRAINER — to train your attention to the first intuitive answer.

  • HEAD & HEART — to make decisions more consciously.

  • PHONE DETOX — to reduce the distracting pull of your phone and take your time back.

Gradually, other projects followed, focused on habits, everyday tasks and personal growth.

My core principle

I'm convinced that change doesn't always require complex systems or hours of practice. Sometimes it's enough to notice a thought, take a few mindful breaths, pause before a habitual reaction or take one small action.

That's why my books and apps are built around simple, practical steps. My goal is to help people get lost in their thoughts less, understand themselves better and find more calm and clarity in everyday life.

I keep learning, experimenting, writing and creating new tools. Because working on yourself isn't a destination — it's a path that continues every single day.

Don't pay with today for a future that doesn't exist yet.Life is happening now.And right now is when you can take the next step.
