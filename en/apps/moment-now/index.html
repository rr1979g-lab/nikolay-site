Moment Now — stop overthinking and return to the present

Moment Now is a practical mindfulness app by Nikolay Shcherbakov. Short exercises help you pause, step out of the stream of thoughts and focus on what is happening right now.

Download on Google Play

Android app. There is no iPhone version yet. · Free

How to stop overthinking and live in the moment

Overthinking rarely feels like a choice. A worry about tomorrow or a replay of yesterday starts on its own, and before you know it half an hour has gone. Arguing with these thoughts usually makes them louder. What helps is a simple, repeatable way to bring attention back to the present.

Moment Now gives you exactly that: simple exercises for pausing, focusing on your breathing, noticing your thoughts and building everyday awareness.

Features

According to its Google Play listing, the app includes:

  • Mindfulness practices
  • Breathing exercises
  • A meditation timer
  • Present-moment exercises
  • Progress tracking
  • Simple daily practices

When and where to use it

You don't need a special room or an hour of free time. The practices are designed for use at home, on a break at work, before sleep, or any moment you notice your mind running ahead. The app is available in English and Russian.

Moment Now is a tool for mindfulness and personal development. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

How to start

  • Install Moment Now from Google Play on your Android phone.
  • Choose one simple practice and do it right now, wherever you are.
  • Use the meditation timer when you want a longer, quiet session.
  • Come back to a short practice whenever your thoughts start spinning.
  • Check your progress to see how the daily habit is growing.

Free to install

On Google Play the app installs at no cost, and the listing notes that it offers in-app purchases.

Why the author created it

For more than 20 years Nikolay Shcherbakov has practised meditation, breathwork and attention techniques. One question kept returning: why do we worry so much about a future that hasn't arrived yet? His book What If Everything Turns Out Well? is about exactly that anxiety and the skill of bringing attention back to the present, and Thoughts in Circles shows how to step out of looping thoughts without arguing with them.

He also saw that a book alone is not enough: we read, feel inspired, and a few days later slip back into old patterns. Moment Now was created to carry that practice into everyday life, so a short exercise is always one tap away. As the author puts it, life is happening now.

Questions and answers

How can I stop overthinking quickly?

A short practice that brings attention back to the present, such as focusing on your breath or simply noticing a thought without continuing it, can interrupt the loop. Moment Now offers simple exercises for exactly these moments.

Is Moment Now free?

The app installs from Google Play at no cost, and the listing states that it contains in-app purchases.

Does Moment Now have a meditation timer?

Yes. Along with mindfulness and breathing exercises, the app includes a meditation timer and progress tracking.

What languages is the app available in?

Moment Now is available in English and Russian.

Can the app replace therapy?

No. The app is intended for mindfulness and personal development and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
