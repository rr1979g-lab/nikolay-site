PILL REMINDER — never miss an important reminder

Forget to take your pills or keep losing track of daily routines? PILL REMINDER is a simple tool for creating schedules and getting reminders right on time.

Download on Google Play

Android app. There is no iPhone version yet.

Screenshots

Why it is so easy to forget your pills

When the day is busy, small regular tasks are the first to slip: a tablet in the morning, vitamins after lunch, a routine you promised yourself to keep. Sticky notes get lost, and memory is not reliable when there is a lot on your mind.

A reminder app takes this load off your head. You set the schedule once, and your phone reminds you at the right time, so you can focus on your day.

What PILL REMINDER does

  • Create schedules for pills, vitamins and other regular tasks.
  • Get timely reminders according to your schedule.
  • Keep track of scheduled reminders in one place.
  • Make everyday routines easier to manage.

Who it is for

PILL REMINDER is for anyone who takes medicine or supplements on a schedule, for people who help an older relative remember their routine, and for those who simply want reliable reminders for important daily tasks. It is meant to be simple and easy to use.

It also helps beyond medicine: drinking water, a short stretching break, a daily walk or a few minutes of calm. If you like short pauses during the day, the app MOMENT NOW offers practices that bring your attention back to the present.

How to start

  • Install PILL REMINDER from Google Play.
  • Add what you need to remember — for example, a pill or a daily routine.
  • Set the schedule and times that match your day or your doctor’s instructions.
  • Allow notifications so reminders arrive on time.
  • Check your list from time to time and update it when your schedule changes.

Important safety note

PILL REMINDER is a reminder tool only. It does not replace a doctor and does not give medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist about which medicine to take, how much and when, and ask them if you have any questions.

Questions and answers

How can I remember to take my pills every day?

Set a schedule in a reminder app and allow notifications. PILL REMINDER lets you create schedules and get reminders on time.

Can I use PILL REMINDER for things other than pills?

Yes. It is a tool for schedules and timely reminders, so you can use it for any important daily routine.

Does the app tell me what medicine to take?

No. The app only reminds you according to the schedule you set. Decisions about medicine and dosage belong to your doctor.

Where can I download PILL REMINDER?

PILL REMINDER is available for Android on Google Play.
