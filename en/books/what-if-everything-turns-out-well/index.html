What If Everything Turns Out Well? — a new way to see the future

We often imagine worst-case scenarios that may never happen. This book helps you change how you think about the future and stop paying with today for something that hasn’t happened yet.

What the book is about

How many times have you lived through something that never actually happened? Work, money, health, relationships, the people you love — an anxious mind can turn any of them into a bad scenario. Sometimes we even rehearse conversations that never take place.

What If Everything Turns Out Well? is about the habit of living through bad outcomes in advance. It teaches you to tell a real problem from an anxious scenario, to notice the moment a thought turns into a mental movie about the future, and to come back to the facts — to what you can actually influence right now.

Its central message: the future hasn’t happened yet, and uncertainty works both ways. It can hold good things, not only bad ones.

What you will learn

  • How to tell a real problem from an anxious story that exists only in your head.
  • How to notice when a thought about tomorrow has become a film with a bad ending.
  • How to return to facts instead of guesses and assumptions.
  • How to focus on what you can do today.
  • How to notice good possibilities, not just risks.

Practices for anxious thoughts

The book includes short practices for working with anxious thoughts, attention, the body and breathing. They need no special preparation and fit into an ordinary day — on the way to work, before an important meeting, or at night when your thoughts won’t let you sleep.

If breathing exercises work for you, you can continue them with the Breathing Practices app. If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable during a breathing exercise, stop and breathe normally.

Who this book is for

It is for anyone tired of worrying about problems that haven’t even happened, anyone used to preparing for the worst “just in case”, and anyone who notices that waiting for trouble steals the joy from the present.

It is practical reading based on the author’s personal experience, not a medical guide. If anxiety seriously affects your life, please talk to a qualified professional.

Questions and answers

How do I stop always expecting the worst?

The book suggests noticing when a thought turns into an anxious scenario, returning to the facts and focusing on what you can influence right now.

Does the book include practical exercises?

Yes, short practices for working with anxious thoughts, attention, the body and breathing.

Where can I buy What If Everything Turns Out Well?

The English edition is on Amazon, and the Russian edition is on Litres.

Is this book a treatment for anxiety?

No. It is practical reading based on personal experience and does not replace professional help. If anxiety is severe, please see a psychologist or doctor.
