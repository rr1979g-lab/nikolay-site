Thoughts in Circles — how to stop the loop of intrusive thoughts

What Thoughts in Circles is about

You replay the same conversation, decision, mistake or message again and again. It feels like one more round of thinking will finally bring the answer. It rarely does — and your peace of mind wears thinner each time.

This book explains how ordinary reflection turns into endless rumination, and how to stop feeding the loop. It draws a clear line between solving a problem and re-analyzing it for the tenth time. It also looks at why we keep re-checking things we already know, and why we chase final answers to questions that may not have one.

The core idea fits in one line: “A thought doesn’t need to be defeated. You can simply not continue it.”

How to stop overthinking: the method in short

Instead of arguing with your own head, the book offers a short sequence you can use in everyday life:

  • notice that you are going in circles again;
  • name what is happening;
  • work out whether you are facing a fact, a question or a repetition;
  • answer once — or not at all;
  • move on to your next action.

What you will learn

  • Why searching for the “final” answer often makes anxious rumination worse.
  • How to reduce your involvement in intrusive thoughts without trying to suppress them.
  • How to tell problem-solving apart from repeated analysis.
  • How to bring your attention back to the present when your mind keeps pulling you away.
  • How to build the new habit through practical exercises and a 30-day program.

Who this book is for

It is for people who get stuck in doubt, struggle to let go of past decisions, reread old messages or keep replaying what someone said. If your thinking drains your energy without adding clarity, this book was written with you in mind.

It is not a psychology textbook but a practical guide built on the author’s own experience. If intrusive thoughts seriously affect your daily life, please talk to a qualified professional — the book does not replace professional help.

Questions and answers

How do I stop intrusive thoughts that keep coming back?

The book suggests not arguing with the thought. Notice the loop, name what is happening, decide whether it is a fact, a question or a repetition, answer once or not at all, and move on to your next action.

Does the book include exercises?

Yes. It includes practical exercises and a 30-day program to help you practice the approach day by day.

Is Thoughts in Circles available in English?

Yes, the English edition is available on Amazon. The Russian edition is sold on Litres.

Can this book replace therapy?

No. It is a practical guide based on the author’s personal experience. If intrusive thoughts seriously affect your life, please see a qualified professional.
