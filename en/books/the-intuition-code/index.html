The Intuition Code — how to hear your inner signals and trust yourself

A book on developing intuition in practice through observation, exercises and personal experience: how to notice inner signals, tell the first sense apart from the doubts that follow, and build self-trust.

Buy on Litres

Russian-language edition

What The Intuition Code is about

Have you ever brushed off a gut feeling — and regretted it later? Intuition often speaks first, but its voice is quickly drowned out by reasoning, fears and endless what-ifs. Over time we stop trusting ourselves and make choices that don’t feel like our own.

The Intuition Code treats intuition not as a mysterious gift but as a skill you can develop. Drawing on his own experience, the author suggests working with it through observation and exercises: learning to notice the first signal, understand it and check your sense.

How to develop intuition: what you will learn

  • How to notice your first inner signals before doubt drowns them out.
  • How to tell the first sense apart from the thoughts and worries that follow.
  • How to develop intuition through self-observation and regular exercises.
  • How to check your sense against your own experience.
  • How to build self-trust step by step and make decisions with more confidence.

Exercises and practice

The focus of the book is practice. You cannot develop intuition just by reading about it: you need to observe, try and notice the results. That is why the book includes exercises you can fit into an ordinary day to gradually learn to hear yourself better.

For daily training the author also created the Intuition Trainer app: short interactive exercises that help you notice the first signal, check your sense and track your progress.

Who this book is for

It is for people who can’t hear their inner voice or don’t dare to trust it, who second-guess their decisions and later wish they had listened to their first impression. It will also interest anyone who simply wants to understand themselves and their reactions better.

About the author

Nikolay Shcherbakov writes books about thinking, intuition, mindfulness and personal change. For more than 20 years he has studied and practiced meditation, breathwork, affirmations, attention techniques and self-observation. One question has always drawn him: can we learn to hear our intuition more clearly? In his words, The Intuition Code helps you learn to notice the first inner signal.

Questions and answers

How do I start developing my intuition?

The book suggests starting with observation: notice the first inner signal and learn to tell it apart from the doubts that come after it. Then reinforce the skill with exercises.

Does the book include intuition exercises?

Yes. Intuition is developed in practice — through observation, exercises and personal experience.

How can I tell intuition from fear?

A key skill in the book is separating the first sense from the doubts that follow. The author’s book The Right Choice goes deeper into telling fear from intuition when making decisions.

Is The Intuition Code available in English?

Not yet. The book is currently available in Russian on Litres.
