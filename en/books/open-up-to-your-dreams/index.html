Open Up to Your Dreams — how intuition guides the right decisions

A book based on 20 years of the author’s experience: how to listen to your intuition, trust yourself and make choices that lead toward your dreams.

Buy on Litres

Russian-language edition

What Open Up to Your Dreams is about

Many of us put our dreams off until “later”. Not because they don’t matter, but because we are afraid of failing, choosing the wrong path or getting it wrong. Doubts pile up, and over time a dream becomes something it is easier not to think about.

This book is about how intuition helps you make decisions that lead to what truly matters. Drawing on 20 years of experience, Nikolay Shcherbakov shows how to listen to your inner voice, trust yourself and make choices out of understanding what you want rather than out of fear.

What you will learn

  • How to listen to your intuition in everyday and important decisions.
  • How to trust yourself when fear and doubt hold you back.
  • How to overcome the hesitation that keeps you from moving forward.
  • How to make choices that lead toward your dreams.
  • How to take your first steps toward what really matters to you.

How intuition helps you make the right decisions

Intuition often points the way before we have time to justify everything logically. But if you are not used to hearing it, that first signal easily gets lost among worries and other people’s opinions. The book helps you bring your attention back to this inner compass and use it when you are choosing between the familiar and what you truly want.

You can also practice noticing the first signal during the day — the author created the Intuition Trainer app with short interactive exercises for exactly that.

Who this book is for

It is for people who have set their dreams aside because they fear failure or the wrong path, for those standing on the edge of change and not daring to take the step, and for anyone who wants to learn to trust themselves more. It also suits readers who are just beginning to explore intuition.

About the author

Nikolay Shcherbakov writes books about thinking, intuition, mindfulness and personal change. For more than 20 years he has studied and practiced meditation, breathwork, affirmations, attention techniques and self-observation. His core principle: change doesn’t always require complex systems — sometimes it is enough to notice a thought, take a few mindful breaths or take one small action.

Where to buy

Open Up to Your Dreams is currently available in Russian on Litres, under the title «Откройся своим мечтам». An English edition is not available yet. If you read English, the author’s What If Everything Turns Out Well? is available on Amazon.

Questions and answers

How do I learn to trust my intuition?

The book suggests listening to your inner voice, noticing when fear is driving a decision, and gradually building self-trust through your own experience.

How do I stop putting off my dreams out of fear?

The author shows how to overcome hesitation and take first steps toward what matters, relying on intuition rather than fear of failure.

How is it different from The Intuition Code?

The Intuition Code focuses on noticing and checking inner signals. Open Up to Your Dreams is about using intuition to make choices that lead toward your dreams.

Is Open Up to Your Dreams available in English?

Not yet. The book is currently available in Russian on Litres.
