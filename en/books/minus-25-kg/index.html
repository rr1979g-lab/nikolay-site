Minus 25 kg — and a Face Without Wrinkles! A personal diet story

15 years without my usual food. An honest personal story of how the author lost 25 kg in 2 months — with a breakdown of mistakes and no promises of a magic diet.

Buy on Litres

Russian-language edition

What this book is about

Extra weight, exhausting diets that never last, worries about aging — so many people know these struggles. This book is not another weight-loss system but the author’s personal story: how he lost 25 kg, changed his eating habits and rethought how he cares for his appearance.

For more than 15 years Nikolay Shcherbakov has lived without many everyday foods: no coffee, no sugar, no flour, no meat and no fish. In the book he honestly describes how he came to this and which mistakes he made along the way.

What you will find inside

  • A personal story: how the author lost 25 kg in 2 months.
  • 15 years of experience living without coffee, sugar, flour, meat and fish.
  • An honest breakdown of the mistakes he made.
  • Observations on how habits, well-being and one’s relationship with the body change.
  • A personal take on caring for your looks.

A personal story, not medical advice

Please keep in mind that this is one person’s account of his own path. The book is not a diet plan, a treatment program or medical advice, and it does not promise that anyone will get the same results. Every body is different.

Before changing your diet, cutting out food groups or trying to lose weight quickly, be sure to consult a doctor. This is especially important if you have a chronic condition, are pregnant, or are a teenager or an older adult.

Who might enjoy this book

Readers tired of diets that only work for a while who want a real story from someone who has lived with a changed way of eating for many years. Readers curious about giving up coffee, sugar, flour, meat and fish — with all the difficulties and mistakes involved. And anyone looking for inspiration to pay closer attention to their own habits.

About the author

Nikolay Shcherbakov writes books about thinking, intuition, habits and personal change. He has practiced meditation, breathwork and self-observation for more than 20 years, and changing the way he eats holds a special place in his life. In his words, this path has let him observe how habits, well-being and his relationship with his own body change.

For everyday cooking he also created the Recipes From Your Fridge app, which helps you find recipes from what you already have at home.
