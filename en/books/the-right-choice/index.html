The Right Choice — how to decide when mind and heart disagree

A practical approach to weighing options, understanding what you really want and overcoming indecision. Less doubt, more confidence in important life decisions.

Buy on Litres

Russian-language edition

What The Right Choice is about

Do you agonize over every important decision, afraid of getting it wrong? Your mind lists one set of arguments, your feelings point somewhere else, and the choice gets put off for weeks. Sometimes you just wish someone would decide for you.

The Right Choice is about exactly those moments — when fear and doubt get in the way of making a decision. The author offers a practical approach: calmly weigh your options, figure out what you really want and learn to separate fear from intuition.

How to make a decision when you’re unsure: what you will learn

  • How to weigh options without getting stuck in endless back-and-forth.
  • How to understand your own wishes rather than other people’s expectations.
  • How to tell fear from intuition when they say different things.
  • How to find clarity when your mind and your feelings give different answers.
  • How to overcome indecision and make important choices with more confidence.

When your mind and your feelings disagree

The hardest decisions are the ones with no obviously right option. Logic says you should, yet something inside resists. Or the other way round: your heart pulls one way while your head finds dozens of reasons to stop. The book helps you look at such situations honestly and stop treating every choice as a risk of making an irreversible mistake.

If you prefer to work through a decision step by step, try the Decision Helper app: it helps you look at the situation from different sides, answer a few questions and compare the arguments.

Who this book is for

It is for anyone afraid of making the wrong choice, anyone who hesitates for a long time and postpones important decisions — about work, moving, relationships or their own plans. It will also help people who decide quickly but then spend ages wondering whether they did the right thing.

About the author

Questions and answers

How do I decide when my head and heart say different things?

The book suggests weighing your options, understanding what you truly want and separating fear from intuition, so it becomes clearer what lies behind each option.

How can I stop being afraid of making the wrong choice?

The author shows how to notice where fear is holding a decision back and how to find enough clarity to act with more confidence.

How is it different from The Intuition Code?

The Intuition Code is about noticing inner signals and trusting yourself. The Right Choice applies that to concrete decisions when fear and doubt get in the way.

Is The Right Choice available in English?

Not yet. The book is currently available in Russian on Litres.
