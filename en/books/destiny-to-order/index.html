Destiny to Order — the affirmation method that changed my life

A book about the power of daily intentions, attention to your goals and consciously shaping your thinking habits. Inside: explanations, personal experience, practical tasks and a 30-day program.

Buy on Litres

Russian-language edition

What Destiny to Order is about

Negative thoughts and limiting beliefs have a way of pulling us back into the same old patterns. We want change, yet our usual way of thinking keeps returning us to familiar scripts.

In this book Nikolay Shcherbakov shares his many years of experience with affirmations — short daily statements that help you direct your attention toward your goals and gradually reshape your thinking. It is not a collection of pretty phrases but a description of a method the author has tested on himself.

The book explains how affirmation work is built, illustrates it with examples from the author’s own life and gives you practical tasks so you can try the method straight away.

What you will learn

  • How daily intentions work and why consistency matters.
  • How to focus your attention on your own goals rather than on fears and limits.
  • How to consciously build helpful thinking habits.
  • How to put affirmations into practice with the tasks in the book.
  • How to complete a 30-day program and make the skill stick.

How to start with affirmations

The method is built around simple, regular steps. You can start small: pick the area of life that matters most to you right now and give it a little attention every day. The 30-day program helps you keep going past the first few days and notice how your view of familiar situations begins to shift.

To keep the practice close at hand, the author also created the Daily Affirmations app, with positive statements and themed practices for confidence, motivation and a mindful approach to your goals.

Who this book is for

It is for anyone who feels stuck in the same life patterns, wants to strengthen self-belief and is looking for a clear, practical way to work with their thinking. It also suits people who have tried affirmations before but never managed to turn them into a habit.

About the author

Nikolay Shcherbakov writes books about thinking, intuition, mindfulness and personal change. For more than 20 years he has studied and practiced meditation, breathwork, affirmations, attention techniques and self-observation. He does not promise instant transformation — he writes to give people clear tools they can practice on their own.

Questions and answers

How do you use affirmations correctly?

The book presents affirmations as a daily practice: attention to your goals, consistency and tasks that help new thinking habits stick, supported by a 30-day program.

Is Destiny to Order available in English?

Not yet. The book is currently available in Russian on Litres.

Does the book include exercises?

Yes. It includes explanations, the author’s personal experience, practical tasks and a 30-day affirmation program.

Is it suitable for beginners?

The book combines explanations of the method with practical tasks and a 30-day program, so you can start even without prior experience with affirmations.
